NORTHSTAR PEOPLE & CULTURE
REMOTE WORK POLICY — VERSION 1.0
Effective: August 18, 2026

1.0 INTRODUCTION
Welcome to remote work at Northstar. We believe that flexibility, trust, and clear communication are key to doing our best work together.

1.4 EQUIPMENT STIPEND
A dedicated workspace helps you stay focused and comfortable. Northstar provides an ergonomic stipend to support your home office setup.

2.1 OFFICE ATTENDANCE
Our hybrid approach asks every team member to work from the office at least two days each week.

3.1 COMMUNICATION PRINCIPLES
We prioritize asynchronous updates for status reports, reserving synchronous meetings for brainstorming, alignment, and problem-solving.

3.3 KNOWLEDGE SHARING
Use team channels for transparent discussions and document decisions in our knowledge base so everyone stays in sync across time zones.

4.1 DATA PROTECTION
Always connect via VPN when working on public networks, keep your devices updated, and lock your screen whenever stepping away.

4.3 PLANNED LEAVE
For planned time away, submit your leave request at least five days in advance so your team can plan ahead.

4.4 WELLBEING AND DISCONNECT
When you take leave, truly disconnect. Turn off notifications and let your designated coverage buddy manage incoming requests.

4.5 EXTENDED LEAVE APPROVAL
Your direct manager reviews and approves extended leave requests. You will receive confirmation by email.

5.1 INTERNAL SUPPORT
Need help with hardware, software, or benefits? The People and IT helpdesks are available 24/7 through our internal portal.

5.3 ONBOARDING ROADMAP
During your first month, complete your 1-on-1 check-ins, join an employee resource group, and review your role expectations.

6.0 SUMMARY
Remember: communicate early, respect working boundaries, and reach out anytime. We are thrilled to have you at Northstar.
